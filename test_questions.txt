6. Edge Cases and Complex Queries
------------------------------
Multi-part Questions:
- What's the temperature trend in Karachi over the last 5 years and how does it compare to global averages?
- Can you analyze rainfall patterns in Hyderabad and suggest agricultural adaptations based on the data?
- How do urban heat islands in Karachi affect local temperatures, and what solutions have been implemented?
- Compare the climate adaptation strategies of coastal vs inland districts and their effectiveness
- What's the correlation between temperature increases and water scarcity in Sindh's agricultural regions?

Hypothetical Scenarios:
- If temperatures rise by 2°C in Sindh by 2050, what would be the impact on agriculture?
- How would increased monsoon intensity affect Karachi's infrastructure?
- What would be the economic impact if sea levels rise by 1 meter in coastal areas?
- If drought frequency doubles, how should farmers adapt their cultivation patterns?
- What emergency measures should be in place if extreme weather events increase by 50%?
